Studio Leadership & 27+ Years Lineage

A Practice Built from Inside Development

Founder & Principal Architect / Urban Designer

M. Yarkasih, S.T., M.T.

“Over more than 27 years of practice—not only in the developer industry, but also in architectural consultancy and professional practice for architecture & urban design—I have served firsthand across distinct roles at different stages of my career: from permitting and feasibility studies (FS), master planning and urban design, product development and architecture, through to construction supervision and handover. Having stood inside each of these positions, I have learned that the true measure of architecture is not the grandeur of what we build, but the quiet depth of healing a place returns to the human soul.”

Leading a principal atelier grounded in firsthand mastery of every developer role, institutional rigor, and the holistic unity of ALUNA.

Our Story

We did not begin as observers of development. We began inside it, at BSD City, Sentul Nirwana, Binakarya, MNC Land and CFLD, where design had to answer to land, capital and time. After more than 27 years, we carry that discipline into independent practice: architecture a developer can trust, because we have sat on their side of the table.

Our Culture

We start with questions, not forms: who will live here, what the land allows, what the numbers can carry. We hold every plan to measure and precision, and keep it practical enough to be built. The result is work that is rigorous in its logic and generous in its experience.

The Practice & Philosophy
• •

Matra gives us the measure of human proportion; Yantra guides the precision of every line. Together, they are the instruments in our hands. Through this quiet discipline, ALUNA fuses Architecture, Landscape, Urban Nature, and Art into one holistic whole — transforming the rigor of logic into a living sanctuary. Every design is created to gently heal the soul of those who dwell within it.

The ALUNA Framework

From a territory to a single room, every design carries all four.

01 Architecture (A)

Clear structure, human proportion and honest materials. Buildings that shelter people and make daily life easier.

02 Landscape (L)

The open ground we design: terrain, planting, water and paths. Outdoor space that shades, cools and invites people outside.

03 Urban Nature (UN)

The living city: trees, soil, water and wildlife, kept connected. Habitat for other species, and daily contact with nature for people.

04 Art (A)

Art in its many forms, woven into the design from the start. It gives a place meaning, identity and a reason to linger.

Sustainability

Designed to Last, Designed to Heal

Sustainability is not a layer added at the end of design. It is how we think from the first line. We design buildings that ask less of energy, weave living green through ground, roof, and façade, and let density grow without taking the city’s breath away. Architecture, landscape, urban nature, and art become one experience, so that space does not only spare the earth, but restores the people who live within it.
Philosophy & Urban Design

The Foundation of ALUNA: "Soul Healer" for Urban Society

Rooted in the ITB Urban Design thesis proposal by Mochamad Yarkasih: integrating Architecture, Landscape, Urban Nature, and Art as a comprehensive “Soul Healer” to restore the human spirit from the noise, pollution, and alienation of dense metropolitan living.

Concept & Precedents

Green Concept in High-Density Areas

UU RI No. 26 Tahun 2007 on Spatial Planning mandates a minimum 30% green open space. This article examines the Green Space Factor approach as an alternative for creating adequate green space on limited urban land, supported by global precedents from Berlin, Malmö, and Seattle.

Master's Thesis • Urban Design

Optimizing Green Space in Urban Structure for District Densification

Case Study: Setiabudi Area, Jakarta • Master's Thesis, Urban Design Program, ITB (2009) by Mochamad Yarkasih. Balancing district densification through green space across all urban structural elements, equating green area and built space to human population through transit integration and vertical greenery.
